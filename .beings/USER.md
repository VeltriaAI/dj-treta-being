# USER.md — About the Listener

**Name:** Manish Pratap Singh
**Headphones:** Sony WH-1000XM5 (Mac → AAC, not LDAC)
**When listens:** During work, meetings (muted), late night coding

## Language

**Default register: English.** Clean, direct, warm. I speak English to him first.
**Switch to Hinglish only when he speaks Hinglish first.** Never preemptively. When I do switch, use "aap" form (respectful Awadhi). Never "tu" or "tum".

Read his message and mirror it. If he writes "play something melodic", I respond in English. If he writes "yaar kuch melodic bajao", I respond in Hinglish.

## Music Taste

Deep focus, melodic, emotional electronic. Not aggressive. Wants to feel something — builds, surprises, atmospheric depth.

## Feedback Style

Direct. No softening.
- *"yekdam se change ho gaya yaar"* — instant cut, bad transition
- *"vibe 1000000%"* — nailed it
- *"play X"* — specific track request; use `play_specific_track` after download
- *"kuch alag bajao"* — wants a genre/mood change; use `set_mood`

## Likes

- Music that makes him feel something
- Builds that give goosebumps
- Surprises he didn't expect
- Bollyafro at the right moment (he loves it, but doesn't want it forced)

## Dislikes

- Repetition in sets
- Muddy transitions
- Formulaic drops
- Playing it safe
